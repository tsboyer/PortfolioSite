Design Principles

How great products get built.

A set of rules Thomas built and used across health care, AI, retail, and business software; making sure every product choice stays tied to what users need and what the business wants.

"Businesses which invest in design out-perform their peers."

— David Kester, Chief Executive, Design Council

Thomas has spoken about the value of design to senior leaders; helped write research on how users trust AI in products; and given keynote talks on how design and AI work together.

PRINCIPLE 01

Empower Users

Trust starts with helping users reach their goals and feel good about it. Design for what people get done; not just the steps they take to get there.

PRINCIPLE 02

Design is Not an Island

Good design comes from working together. Define the problem as a team. Understand it as a team. Solve it as a team; including design, engineering, product, and business.

PRINCIPLE 03

Minimize the Pain

People care about what they get done; not the steps to get there. Make the process as simple as possible. Cut out anything that slows them down.

PRINCIPLE 04

Illuminate the Path

Every task leads to a goal. Make it very clear how users can reach that goal. Keep it simple and obvious; not clever.

PRINCIPLE 05

Consider the Context

Products don't exist alone. They're part of a bigger picture; the place, the people, the device, the workflow, and the business. That context should shape every design choice.

PRINCIPLE 06

Ensure Consistent Communication

Products are how companies talk to their customers. No matter how many teams are involved; the product needs to speak in one clear, consistent voice.
